# Commercial use

serial-console-mcp is free for hobbyists. Commercial use needs a license.

Starting with the first release after 0.3.3, serial-console-mcp is licensed under the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). Versions up to and including 0.3.3 were
released under the MIT License and stay under it; nothing on this page changes the terms of a
copy you already have.

The [LICENSE](LICENSE) file is the binding text. This page explains it in plain words and lists
what you can buy. If the two ever seem to disagree, or you are not sure which side of the line
you are on, ask before you rely on either.

## Free to use

You do not need a license, a key or a registration to use serial-console-mcp for:

- **Personal use and hobby projects.** Your own equipment, your own lab, your own learning.
- **Amateur radio.** Your station, a club station, Field Day and contests, and volunteer
  emergency communication.
- **Education.** Schools, colleges and universities, their students and teachers, and
  courses that teach networking, radio or embedded systems.
- **Nonprofit and public organizations.** Charitable organizations, public research
  organizations, public safety and health organizations, environmental protection
  organizations and government institutions.

Free use includes changing the software and sharing it, as long as whoever gets a copy also
gets the license and the `Required Notice:` line.

## Needs a commercial license

Any use by a business or for a business needs a commercial license. That includes:

- **Internal use.** Employees configuring the company's routers, switches, radios or other
  equipment, in production, in a lab or on a test bench.
- **Work for clients.** Consultants, contractors, integrators and managed service providers
  using it on customer equipment.
- **Products and services.** Shipping it with hardware, building it into a product, or
  running it as part of a service you sell.
- **Business use on a personal machine.** If the work is for your employer or your
  customers, it needs a license, even on your own laptop and your own time.

If you are not sure, assume a license is needed and ask. Most questions take one email.

## What you can buy

- **Commercial license.** Annual, per company. Covers the company and its employees, with
  every release published during the term.
- **Support for additional devices and protocols.** New device presets, protocol builders
  and parsers, and operating rules for equipment the project does not cover yet, verified
  on your hardware.
- **Custom skills and integrations.** Operating skills written for your equipment, your
  change procedures and your tooling, and integration with the systems around them.
- **Support plans.** Help with deployment, answers when a console does something
  unexpected, and priority fixes.
